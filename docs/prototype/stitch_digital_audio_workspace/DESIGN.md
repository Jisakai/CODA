## Brand & Style

The design system is an ultra-precise, high-performance creative workstation architecture conceived for professional music production, synthesis, and signal sequencing. Built for hardware-grade tactile interaction, the visual identity rejects skeuomorphic kitsch in favor of an unapologetic industrial brutalism: razor-sharp corners, monoline grid precision, and an uncompromised true-black OLED environment.

### Target Persona & Aura
- **Audience:** Sound designers, electronic music producers, modular synthesists, and live performers who demand zero visual lag, high-density telemetry, and rapid spatial recall.
- **Emotional Impact:** Surgical, focused, intimidatingly fast, and mathematically exact. Every pixel represents state or value; non-functional decorative artifacts are banned.
- **Design Movement:** Experimental Monoline Brutalism crossed with Electric Ultraviolet Synthesis. The interface operates like a physical studio console engineered out of dark volcanic glass and ionized gas, where active signals ignite against absolute void.

## Colors

The palette leverages the true-black capabilities of OLED displays, collapsing background illumination entirely to zero luminescence (`#000000`) to maximize optical contrast and prevent touch fatigue in dim studio conditions.

### Palette Architecture
- **Canvas Base (`#000000`):** Complete zero-luminescence black. Defines void space and master canvas boundaries.
- **Surface Elevation 1 (`#0d0e11`):** Matte obsidian plane for audio racks, modular cells, and routing panels.
- **Surface Elevation 2 (`#14161b`):** Recessed slots, parameter strips, and secondary step containers.
- **Structural Rules (`#45474E`):** Monoline 1px strokes used for all grid boundaries, cell dividers, and enclosure edges.
- **Active Signal / Pure State (`#ffffff`):** Dedicated to active operational states, playback playheads, primary values, and selected hardware nodes.
- **Melody & Synthesis Accent (`#a855f7`):** Electric ultraviolet. Acts as the primary signal path, envelope modulators, and lead voice telemetry.
- **Bass & Low-End Accent (`#2dd4bf`):** Ionized cyan/teal. Reserved for sub-oscillators, filter frequencies, and low-frequency parameter tracking.
- **Harmonics & Chord Accent (`#f59e0b`):** Radiant amber/gold. Dictates polyphony arrays, scale overlays, and saturation drive meters.

Use colors strictly as functional data carriers. Elements never receive color purely for decorative backdrop fills; chromatic saturation directly indicates live voltage, audio signal presence, or track assignment.

## Typography

Typography functions as an analytical telemetry readout. The dynamic pairing of **Space Grotesk** and **Geist** bridges mechanical hardware frontplates with modern screen ergonomics.

- **Space Grotesk:** Applied to all rack titles, module headings, octave readouts, and operational values. Its aggressive geometric terminals and technical posture anchor numeric parameters and section demarcation.
- **Geist:** Employed across body text, telemetry sub-labels, input tags, and parameter micro-readouts. Its ultra-neutral, engineered grotesque forms deliver pristine legibility at fractional scales (`0.625rem`) without optical distortion.
- **Numerics & Readouts:** All continuous parameter readouts (decibels, hertz, BPM, ms) must enable tabular lining numerals (`tnum`) to eliminate spatial jitter during real-time automated parameter sweeps.
- **Casing Protocol:** Use uppercase tracking exclusively on `label-sm` and `label-md` for hardware abbreviations (e.g., `OSC 1`, `LFO`, `ENV ATTACK`, `VCF RES`).

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy mirrors an edge-to-edge instrument faceplate. Content spans fluidly across the hardware screen without floating sidebars or detached bottom action sheets.

### Grid Engine
- **Structure:** 16-step modular horizontal micro-grid for step sequences and time-division arrangements; 12-column sub-grid for parameter matrix panels.
- **Gutters & Dividers:** Columns share hairline structural borders (`#45474E`) with zero negative air between adjacent modules. Where physical separation is required, apply `space-sm` (4px) or `space-md` (8px).
- **Edge Margin:** Tight boundaries (`margin: 0.75rem` on mobile/tablet viewports; `margin-desktop: 1rem`). Outer edges kiss the glass perimeter to maximize interactive touch surface for step sequencing and multi-touch fader manipulation.

### Spatial Adaptation
- **Mobile/Compact:** Channels collapse into vertical stack tiers; matrix headers convert to horizontal tab scrolls. Touch areas maintain an uncompromised minimum height of 44px despite tight 1px borders.
- **Tablet (Primary Context):** Continuous side-to-side channel strips. Matrix controllers consume dynamic viewport height without pagination. Split parameter bays dock to opposite edges for thumb ergonomics.
- **Desktop/Expanded:** Dual-tier arrangement showing simultaneous master waveform canvas and micro-step trigger engine with 12px structural gutters.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system completely eliminates diffuse drop shadows, ambient blur cones, and layered skeuomorphic bevels. Visual depth is established strictly through **chromatic luminescence tiers** and **1px monoline containment**.

### Structural Tiers
1. **Layer 0 (Void Ground):** `#000000` - Base chassis, global background, inactive playhead canvas.
2. **Layer 1 (Structural Modules):** `#0d0e11` - Framed by 1px solid `#45474E`. Hosts sequence tracks, channel strips, and filter banks.
3. **Layer 2 (Recessed Sliders & Parameter Beds):** `#14161b` - Inset parameter troughs, waveform display backing, and pad cells.
4. **Layer 3 (Overlay & Diagnostics):** Floating contextual HUDs maintain a `#0d0e11` core with a 1px border of `#a855f7` or `#ffffff`, accompanied by zero ambient shadow blur.

### Illumination & State Signatures
- **Active State:** Elements do not lift off the screen on touch; they illuminate. An engaged pad flips to pure white `#ffffff` or its track color with immediate response.
- **Trigger Flash:** Inactive steps maintain `#14161b` with hairline `#45474E` borders. Upon step activation, the perimeter instantly locks to `1px solid #a855f7` (Melody), `#2dd4bf` (Bass), or `#f59e0b` (Chord).
- **Hairline Precision:** Internal boundaries must always render as crisp 1px strokes; do not use anti-aliased semi-transparent borders thicker than 1px.

## Shapes

The shape system enforces pure rectangular brutality (`roundedness: 0`). 

- **Corners:** Exact 90-degree zero-radius joins across every element: buttons, step triggers, modal frames, fader heads, envelope nodes, and input fields.
- **Philosophy:** Rounded corners waste active screen acreage and dilute the visual metaphor of heavy-duty hardware synthesizers and precision calibration instruments.
- **Perimeter Boundaries:** All components achieve spatial definition through razor-sharp lines and adjacent color shifts. No rounded pills or soft chips are permitted under any condition.

## Components

### Buttons & Switches
- **Trigger Buttons:** Constructed with `#0d0e11` background, 1px border of `#45474E`, text set in `label-md` uppercase. Active or latched state: background turns `#ffffff`, text turns `#000000`.
- **Octave / Directional Step Toggles:** Joined-button groups where inner borders collapse into single 1px `#45474E` lines. The active segment is demarcated by a 1px border in `#a855f7` or full `#14161b` fill with `#ffffff` text.

### Chips & Track Assigners
- **Structure:** Zero-radius rectangular tags.
- **Visuals:** `#000000` background with a 1px colored border matching the channel domain (`#2dd4bf` for Bass, `#f59e0b` for Chords, `#a855f7` for Lead Melody).
- **Typography:** `label-sm` Space Grotesk, bold tracking (+0.08em).

### Input Fields & Value Encoders
- **Parameter Steppers:** Monospaced numerical values set in Space Grotesk. Enclosed in a 1px `#45474E` box with `#14161b` fill.
- **Scrubbing Interaction:** While adjusting, the border transitions from `#45474E` to `#ffffff`. Value readout switches color dynamically based on its parent track.

### Sliders & Touch Strips
- **Track:** Recessed strip of `#14161b`, 1px border `#45474E`. Width is calibrated to precision touch metrics (full height or minimum 32px track width).
- **Fill / Value Level:** Solid geometric block of track accent color (`#2dd4bf`, `#f59e0b`, or `#a855f7`) terminating in a solid 2px `#ffffff` hairline indicator.

### Step Sequencer Matrix & Pads
- **Matrix Cells:** 16-pad arrays constructed of tight zero-gap or 1px hairline-separated grids. Inactive pads sit at `#0d0e11` with `#45474E` internal dividers.
- **Active Step:** Filled with `#14161b` with an inner 2px accent indicator block.
- **Playhead Sweep:** A 1px `#ffffff` vertical rule traversing horizontally across cells with zero latency.

### Audio Cards & Channel Racks
- **Container Structure:** Edge-to-edge docking modules without soft card padding. Module headers are flat bars with `#14161b` background, 1px bottom border of `#45474E`, containing category title in `headline-sm` Space Grotesk.
- **Grouping:** Grouped racks use single hairline dividing lines to prevent double-border thickness artifacts.
